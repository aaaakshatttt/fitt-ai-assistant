# FITT — Knowledge Base

## Organization Overview

FITT stands for Foundation for Innovation and Technology Transfer.

FITT is an industry-academia interface organization established by IIT Delhi. It facilitates research translation, technology development, intellectual property rights (IPR) management, technology transfer and commercialization, R&D collaboration, startup incubation, and mentoring.

FITT aims to act as an effective interface between industry and IIT Delhi to foster, promote, and sustain the commercialization of science and technology for mutual benefit.

## History

FITT was established by the Board of Governors of the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi) as a registered society on 9 July 1992.

Dr. NC Nigam was the founding Chairman of FITT, and Dr. AK Sengupta was the first full-time Managing Director.

The establishment of FITT followed the recommendation of the IIT Delhi Review Committee Report of 1986, which recommended setting up an industrial foundation to strengthen interaction and collaboration between IIT Delhi and industry.

## Intellectual Property and Technology Transfer

FITT manages intellectual property activities of IIT Delhi.

IP management can involve:

- Patents
- Copyrights
- Trademarks
- Trade secrets
- Designs
- Other forms of intellectual property

FITT also facilitates commercialization of IIT Delhi's intellectual property assets and know-how through technology transfer and licensing.

Technology transfer involves transferring scientific and technological research from one entity to another for further development and commercialization.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is FITT?
FITT is the Foundation for Innovation and Technology Transfer at IIT Delhi. It acts as an interface between IIT Delhi and industry to support research, innovation, technology transfer, commercialization, and entrepreneurship.

### Where is FITT located?
FITT is located at IIT Delhi, Hauz Khas, New Delhi - 110016, India.

### When was FITT established?
FITT was established as a registered society on 9 July 1992.

### What does FITT do?
FITT facilitates R&D collaboration, intellectual property management, technology transfer, commercialization, startup incubation, mentoring, professional development, and industry partnerships.

### Does FITT support startups?
Yes. FITT supports technology-based startups through incubation, mentoring, technological support, networking, and other forms of assistance.

### Does FITT work with industry?
Yes. FITT facilitates collaboration between IIT Delhi and industry through R&D partnerships, technology development, consultancy, technology transfer, and other activities.

### What is technology transfer?
Technology transfer is the process of transferring research, technology, intellectual property, or know-how from one organization or entity to another for further development, application, or commercialization.
